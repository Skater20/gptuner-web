Delete Your Account

How to request deletion of your account and data

How to request it

Write to soporte@getmagicrent.com, from the same email address you use to sign in to GPTuner (Google, Apple or email/password), with the subject "Delete GPTuner account". For security, we only process the request from that address; if you can't access it, we'll ask for another way to verify the account is yours before deleting it.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your study settings synced to our server (instrument, tempo, tuning reference, favorites and accents)

We complete the deletion within a maximum of 30 days of your request, and confirm it by email once done.

What is not affected

Your recordings and training progress live only on your phone: they are removed when you uninstall the app, independently of this request.

Delete your account

How to request deletion of your Metonik account and its data.

How to request it

Email support@metonik.com with the subject "Delete Metonik account" and tell us the email you sign in with (Google, Apple or email and password).

Because an email sender can be forged, that message alone is never enough to delete anything or reveal any data: before processing the request we send a single-use confirmation link to the address registered on the account, and we only delete it once it is confirmed from there.

We complete the deletion within 30 days of confirmation and let you know by email when it is done.

Request deletion

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your profile on our server: email, name, photo, sign-in history and usage time.
  • Your synced practice settings.
  • The reports you sent from the app.

We only keep, in a limited way, the security logs the law allows us to retain to protect the service.

What this request does not cover

Your recordings, training progress and workspaces live only on your phone: they are removed when you uninstall the app.

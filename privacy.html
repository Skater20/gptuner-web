METONIK

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Controller

The controller for the data of Metonik (the app and this website) is Alejandro Luque. For any privacy question, write to support@metonik.com.

2. Data we process

  • Account. When you sign in with Google, Apple or email and password (Firebase Authentication) we process your email address, the account identifier, the sign-in method and, if the provider shares them, your name and profile photo.
  • Account activity. Sign-up date, sign-ins, last activity, platform (Android or iOS), app version and the time you spend using the app in the foreground. We use it to run the service, find bugs and understand which features are used.
  • Practice settings. If you sync your account we store your instrument, tempo, reference pitch, favourites and metronome accents so you can restore them on another device.
  • Reports and suggestions. If you send a report from the app we store the message, its category, your email, the app version, the platform and basic device details.
  • Security. For suspicious or abusive requests we log the IP address, the requested path, the browser or client and the time, to protect the service.
  • Microphone. The tuner, ear training and other tools analyse sound in real time on your device. That audio is never recorded or sent to any server.
  • Recordings and workspaces. Recordings, training progress and your workspace setups are stored only on your device. If you export a setup, you control that file.
  • Test access request (website). If you fill in the form on this website we process your name, email, device type and language, only to manage your access to testing.

3. Why we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account.
  • To sync your settings across devices.
  • To provide and improve the app's tools.
  • To handle your reports, suggestions and support requests.
  • To manage pre-launch testing.
  • To protect the service against abuse and unauthorised access.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request, our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and running and, where applicable, your consent (for example, when you send the test access form).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or show ads. We only use the technical providers needed to run the service:

  • Google Firebase, for authentication (and Google or Apple if you sign in with them).
  • Render and Supabase, to host the app's server and database.
  • Cloudflare, to host and protect this website.
  • FormSubmit, which delivers the test access form requests to us by email.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; when they do, it is under the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

Account data, while the account is active. Reports and security logs, for as long as needed to handle them and protect the service. Test access requests, until launch or until you ask us to delete them. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to or restrict its processing, by writing to support@metonik.com. If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

7. Deleting your account

You can request full deletion of your account and its data on this page.

8. Children

Metonik is not intended for children under 14 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown above.

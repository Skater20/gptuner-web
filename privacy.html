Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Data controller

GPTuner is operated by Alejandro Luque. For any privacy question, write to soporte@getmagicrent.com.

2. What data we process

  • Account: when you sign in with Google, Apple or email/password (Firebase Authentication) we process your email address and account identifier.
  • Study settings: if you sync your account, we store your instrument, tempo, tuning reference, favorites and metronome accents, so they follow you across devices.
  • Microphone: used to detect your instrument's pitch in real time, entirely on your own phone. Tuner audio is never recorded or sent to any server.
  • Recordings: if you use the recorder, audio files are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Training progress: stars, XP, achievements and streaks are stored locally on your phone; they are not synced to our servers today.

3. Why we use this data

  • To let you sign in and keep your session
  • To sync your study settings across devices
  • To provide the tuner, metronome, training and recorder
  • To support you when you contact us

Legal basis: performance of the service you request and, where applicable, your consent when creating an account.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use only the technical providers strictly necessary to run the service: Firebase (Google) for authentication, and a cloud host for the settings-sync backend. We never share data with advertisers; GPTuner shows no ads.

5. How long we keep data

For as long as your account stays active. Recordings and training progress live on your device and are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure or portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to soporte@getmagicrent.com. If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Account deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and synced settings on this page.

8. Children

GPTuner is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided us data, contact us to have it removed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the latest revision.

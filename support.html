Support

Got a problem or an idea? We are here to help.

Write to support@metonik.com with your device and OS version, your Metonik version, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps if you can add one. You can also send a report from inside the app. We reply within 3-5 business days.

Frequently asked questions

When does Metonik launch?

It is in development and will be out soon for Android and iPhone. Meanwhile you can request test access.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The tuner, metronome, training and recorder work without internet. You only need a connection to sign in and sync settings.

Is what the microphone hears recorded?

No. Sound is analysed in real time on your device and never sent to a server. Audio is only saved if you use the recorder, and it stays on your device.

Are there ads?

No. Metonik does not show ads.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the account deletion page.

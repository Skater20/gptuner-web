Frequently asked questions

Does the tuner work without an internet connection?
Yes, the tuner, metronome, training and recorder work fully offline. You only need a connection to sign in and sync settings across devices.

Does GPTuner show ads?
No. GPTuner shows no advertising.

Where are my recordings stored?
Only on your phone. They are never uploaded to any server.

How do I delete my account?
You can request it on the account deletion page.

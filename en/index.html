01 / WORKSPACE

One workspace. Your way of making music.

Workspace brings Metonik’s tools together on one screen. Up to three blocks per desktop, multiple desktops and a layout you control.

01

Choose your tools

Add the blocks you need: tuner, note, instrument, metronome, scales, drone, pitch graph or recording.

02

Make room for what matters

Reorder and resize blocks, minimize them or open one full screen. Swipe between desktops.

03

Return to your setup

Save named configurations for long tones, technique or rhythm. Recall them for your next practice session.

↗

Your next session starts here.

Save your layout and practice settings. Export and import configurations to keep or share them.

02 / MORE WAYS TO PRACTISE

01

Tuner

Choose a fast, balanced or smooth response. Polyphonic tuning for up to four notes and different temperaments help you explore how sounds fit together.

02

Metronome

Time signatures, accents, subdivisions, world rhythms and bar programs to practise tempo changes and musical structure.

03

Drone

Hold a reference tone with your selected instrument’s timbre. Listen, play along and work on intonation and intervals.

04

Percussion

Drum kit, timpani, marimba, xylophone, vibraphone and glockenspiel. Visual guides and tailored practice; microphone hit identification is experimental.
